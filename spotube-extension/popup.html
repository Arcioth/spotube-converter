YouTube Music Playlist Creator

Security & Consent Authorization

Before proceeding, you must authorize this extension to run locally in your browser.

How this keeps you safe:
  • No External Servers: This extension runs 100% locally on your machine. Your data is NEVER sent to any 3rd-party servers, databases, or analytics.
  • INNERTUBE_API_KEY & SAPISIDHASH: YouTube requires these internal tokens to create playlists. This extension securely reads them from your active YouTube Music tab in memory and immediately uses them to construct the payload for YouTube. They never leave your browser context.
  • Permissions Justification:
    • Tabs & Scripting: To open a background YouTube Music tab and securely inject the local migration script.
    • Storage: To save your log history and this consent agreement.
